Membership

HolyHim membership opens every lesson. It is a yearly subscription through Apple that renews automatically: $1.99 a year in the US, shown in your local currency before you confirm. New members can start with a free week; Apple decides who is eligible.

Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm, or when the free week ends if you start with one. Membership renews each year at the price shown unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that end. To cancel a free week without being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

You can manage or cancel membership at any time in your Apple account settings: Settings, your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep membership until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your HolyHim account does not cancel it. Restore purchases recovers membership on the same Apple account. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.

How lessons open

One new lesson opens each calendar day after you finish the one before. Missed days do not pile up, and completed lessons stay open to read again.

Your account

Your account keeps your progress, saved verses, settings and community card in sync across your devices. You can export your data or delete your account from Profile. Features that contact our server need an internet connection. Our privacy policy explains the information we use.

Community rules

The Brothers community is for encouragement. By joining it you agree to these rules. Be real: share what is true for you. Be kind: no mocking, bullying, harassment, hate or slurs. Keep it clean: no sexual content, nudity, violence, threats, self-harm encouragement, illegal activity, spam, selling or links. Protect each other: report anything that breaks these rules, and if someone may be in danger, contact local emergency services (911 in the US) or the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Every post, reply and name is filtered before it appears. Anyone can report a post or reply or block another member. We review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules, and remove the accounts of people who post it. Content reported by several members is hidden until it is reviewed. You are responsible for what you post, and you keep ownership of it, but you allow us to show it to other members inside HolyHim. You can delete it at any time.
