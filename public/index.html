A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Room for
your questions.

Still wondering about something?
We're here to help ↗

01How does lifetime access work?

After onboarding, lifetime access is a one-time in-app purchase on iPhone or iPad. Verified purchases and existing retained-access grants are recognized. There is no subscription.

02Do I need to know the Bible already?

No. Each day includes a short explanation of the verse in its setting. Whether this is your first reading or a familiar habit, you can begin as you are.

03How does a new day open?

Finish today's four cards and the next lesson opens on the following calendar day. Missed days never build into a backlog. Completed lessons stay available to read again.

04What happens if I miss a day?

Your next unread lesson waits for you. Every seven-day run earns a grace day, up to two, to gently protect your streak. There is always room to begin again.

05Which Bible translation do you use?

We use the World English Bible, a public-domain translation. Every quoted verse is kept separate from HolyHim's explanations and prayers.

06Can I read without a connection?

Once you've signed in and loaded your walk, your reading and progress work locally. Changes sync when you're connected again. Artwork has a quiet themed fallback while an image is unavailable.

07Is my progress kept across devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple, Google, or email account to keep your completed lessons, saved verses, and preferences together.
