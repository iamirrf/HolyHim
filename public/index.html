A few things you might be wondering

A little clarity.

Is HolyHer free?+

Yes. The complete 365-day Scripture journey is free to access.

What does a daily reading look like?+

Five reading-only cards: an exact World English Bible passage, its meaning, everyday encouragement, a truth to carry, and an original prayer. Most readings take two to three minutes. There are no quizzes or timers.

Do I need to know the Bible already?+

No. Come with curiosity. Brief, optional personalization helps shape the introductory guidance without changing the Scripture or lesson order.

What if I miss a day?+

Your unlocked readings stay available. You can return, reread, or catch up at your own pace. Missing a day never takes away a badge you’ve earned.

Where can I use HolyHer?+

Read in your browser on your phone, tablet, or computer. Your account keeps your setup, saved moments, and reading progress together. The redesigned iPhone and iPad app is coming soon.

Can I receive a daily Scripture reminder?+

iPhone and iPad reminders are optional and require permission on that device. The web app can save your preferred time; it does not send browser notifications.

How is my information handled?+

Your account stores your preferences, reading progress, and saved items. You can export your data or request account deletion in settings. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
