Finding your next lesson

After you finish a reading, the next lesson opens on the following calendar day. If you miss a day, your next unread lesson waits for you. You can always read completed lessons again from Walk.

Changing your reminder

Open Profile → Daily reminder to choose a time or turn reminders off. If reminders are enabled in HolyHim but do not appear, check notification permission in your device's settings. The web app does not send local device notifications.

Exporting or deleting your account

Open Profile → Export my data to keep a copy of your account information. For deletion, choose Delete account, confirm, and sign in again when asked. Deletion requires a connection. If you cannot sign in, send us a message above so we can help verify ownership.

A free place to begin

Your entire 365-day Scripture journey is free. HolyHim 2.0 has no subscription or payment step. If you have a question about an earlier purchase, include it in your message.
