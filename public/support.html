Free edition access

The complete HolyHer 365 Scripture journey is free to access. One additional lesson unlocks each calendar day. There is no payment or purchase restoration step in the current app. Contact support about any legacy purchase.

Finding your lessons

A new lesson unlocks each day from your journey start. Previously unlocked readings remain available in Walk. If you are offline, reconnect to sync new unlocks across your devices.

Deleting your account

Open HolyHer → Profile → Settings → Delete account. If you cannot sign in, send a request using this form so we can help verify ownership.
