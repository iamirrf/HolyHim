Restoring your purchase

Sign in to HolyHer, then choose Restore Purchases on the purchase screen or in Settings. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Finding your lessons

A new lesson unlocks each day from your journey start. Missed days stay available in Journey. If you are offline, reconnect to sync new unlocks across your devices.

Deleting your account

Open HolyHer → Profile → Settings → Delete account. If you cannot sign in, send a request using this form so we can help verify ownership.
