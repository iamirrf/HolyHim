Support

How can we help?

Tell us what happened and how to reach you. A person reads every message, usually within two days.

We use your email and message only to answer you. Please leave out passwords and payment details. Privacy policy.

When does the next lesson open?

After you finish a lesson, the next one opens on the following calendar day. If you miss days, your next unread lesson simply waits for you.

What is free?

Days 1 to 7 are free. All 365 days are a one-time purchase of $1.99 in the US (shown in your local currency). There is no subscription.

Restoring a purchase

Open Profile and choose Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple account you bought with.

Changing your reminder

Open Profile, then Reminders, to pick a time or turn reminders off. If a reminder does not appear, check notification permission for HolyHim in your device's Settings.

Exporting or deleting your account

Open Profile, then Account. Choose Export my data for a copy, or Delete account to permanently remove your account, progress and saved verses. You may be asked to sign in again first. Deletion needs a connection. If you cannot sign in, send us a message above.
