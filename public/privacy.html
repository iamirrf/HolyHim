Privacy policy

Effective November 2, 2026. HolyHer is operated by Codellion LLC.

Information you choose to share

When you create an account, we process your account identifier, email address, and any name supplied by your chosen sign-in provider. We save the preferred name, Bible familiarity, focus, routine preferences, reminder time, and onboarding answers you provide. Your completed lesson numbers, journey start date, journey time zone, reading completion dates, saved lessons, theme, badges, and reading positions support your daily curriculum and synced progress. Preferences include sounds, haptics, tips, themes, and reading size. Historical quiz selections and attempt results from older clients are retained during migration. We also store a retained edition-access grant separately from legacy purchase records.

Purchases and service providers

The current app has no payment flow or RevenueCat client integration. Apple and RevenueCat may retain genuine legacy transaction records. We do not receive payment-card details. Legacy provider cleanup is processed through a private retry queue so a billing outage does not block HolyHer account deletion. Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud data storage, and backend hosting. These providers also process technical information needed to deliver and protect their services, including unlinked service diagnostics used for reliability analysis. HolyHer does not include advertising or marketing analytics.

Reminders and device storage

On iPhone and iPad, up to 60 daily quote reminders are scheduled locally with your permission and replenished when you return. Your reminder preference is synchronized; permission is specific to each device. The web app does not deliver browser notifications. You can turn them off in HolyHer or your device settings. Account-specific SQLite storage on native devices and transactional IndexedDB storage in the browser preserve your previously unlocked readings when offline. The browser caches the public application shell and lesson assets separately from account data. You can export your account data in Settings.

Support messages

The support form collects your email and message so we can respond. A temporary one-way identifier derived from the request address limits repeated submissions; it is scheduled for automatic removal after 24 hours. Please do not include passwords or payment details in a support message.

How we use and share information

We use this information to operate your account, sync your journey, validate access, provide support, and protect the service. We do not sell your personal information. Service providers process information for these functions. We may disclose information if required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Account data remains while your account is active. You can request account deletion in HolyHer under Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, preferences, saved items, attempts, reading positions, synced journey, and free-edition grant. Where a legacy billing record exists, provider cleanup is queued privately for retry. A server-private one-way account marker prevents delayed billing events from recreating deleted data; it contains no name, email, or lesson history. Apple may retain transaction records under its own policies. Completed account deletion does not refund an App Store purchase. Support records are retained only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations.

Your choices and contact

You can edit your setup, disable reminders, sign out, or delete your account. For access, correction, deletion help, or privacy questions, use our support form. Your data may be processed in the United States and in locations used by our service providers.

Updates

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date.
