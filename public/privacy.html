YOUR TRUST MATTERS

A little clarity
about your privacy.

Effective November 4, 2026 · HolyHim 2.0 · Codellion LLC

HolyHim uses the information you share to keep your Scripture journey, saved verses, and preferences together. We do not sell your personal information. The app has no advertising or marketing analytics.

Your account and your walk

When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we process your account identifier, email address, and any name supplied by that provider. You can choose the name HolyHim uses for you.

We save the heart focuses you select, your reminder preference and time, theme, reading text size, haptics and sound preferences, time zone, birthday, life season, onboarding answers and unfinished step, optional profile photo reference, and onboarding dates. Your completed lesson numbers and their calendar dates support lesson pacing, streaks, badges, and your reading calendar. Saved lesson numbers and save dates let you find your kept verses again.

Local storage and synchronization

Your app reads and saves changes locally through Firebase Firestore's offline cache, then synchronizes account data when a connection is available. An account-specific local snapshot keeps pending edits, onboarding completion, theme, progress, and saved verses available offline. The app also saves your current lesson card and scroll position on this device, keyed to your account, so you can resume a reading. It clears that position after the lesson is completed. The app also caches public lesson content and artwork. Some features, including first sign-in, account deletion, and sending a support message, require a connection.

When an existing account moves to HolyHim 2.0, the app reads its earlier profile and journey dates once to transfer prior progress. Existing account records are included when you delete the account.

Reminders

On supported devices, reminders are local notifications scheduled with your permission. The schedule holds no more than 30 pending reminders. Each reminder shows the calendar-based Verse of the Day, its Scripture reference, and a short excerpt. Opening it does not advance lesson progress. Permission is specific to each device. You can change the time or turn reminders off in HolyHim or in your device settings.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud data storage, backend functions, and website hosting. Apple and Google handle their respective sign-in services. Firebase Storage holds optional profile photos; replaced photos and account photos are removed by our backend. RevenueCat and Apple process purchase identifiers and entitlement information to provide lifetime access and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment card details. These providers process the technical information needed to deliver and protect their services. Your data may be processed in the United States and in locations used by these service providers.

Support messages

The support form collects your email address and message so we can respond. A temporary one-way identifier derived from the request address limits repeated submissions and is scheduled for removal after 24 hours. Please leave passwords and payment details out of support messages.

Keeping and deleting information

Your account data remains while your account is active. You can export your data in Profile. To delete your account, open Profile → Delete account. HolyHim asks you to confirm and sign in again, then removes your account, optional uploaded photos, and cloud records. Purchase transaction records may remain with Apple and RevenueCat under their retention policies. Deletion requires a network connection.

A private deletion guard prevents late writes from recreating deleted account data. Support records are retained as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. We may disclose information when required by applicable law.

Your choices

You can edit your name and preferences, remove saved verses, disable reminders, export data, sign out, or delete your account. For access, correction, deletion help, or privacy questions, use our support form.

Updates to this page

We revise the effective date when these practices change.
