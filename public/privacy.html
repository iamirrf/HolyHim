Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026. HolyHer is operated by Codellion LLC.

Information you choose to share

When you create an account, we process your account identifier, email address, and any name supplied by your chosen sign-in provider. We save the preferred name, Bible familiarity, focus, routine preferences, reminder time, and onboarding answers you provide. Your completed lesson numbers, journey start date, journey time zone, and activity dates support your daily curriculum and synced progress.

Purchases and service providers

Apple processes App Store payments. We do not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat validates purchases and restores lifetime access using your account identifier and transaction information. Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud data storage, and backend hosting. These providers also process technical information needed to deliver and protect their services, including unlinked service diagnostics used for reliability analysis. HolyHer does not include advertising or marketing analytics.

Reminders and device storage

Reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. You can turn them off in HolyHer or your device settings. Saved lessons and an account-specific progress cache let you return to previously unlocked content when offline.

Support messages

The support form collects your email and message so we can respond. A temporary one-way identifier derived from the request address limits repeated submissions; it is scheduled for automatic removal after 24 hours. Please do not include passwords or payment details in a support message.

How we use and share information

We use this information to operate your account, sync your journey, validate access, provide support, and protect the service. We do not sell your personal information. Service providers process information for these functions. We may disclose information if required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Account data remains while your account is active. You can request account deletion in HolyHer under Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile and synced journey and requests removal of the linked RevenueCat customer record. A server-private one-way account marker prevents delayed billing events from recreating deleted data; it contains no name, email, or lesson history. Apple may retain transaction records under its own policies. Completed account deletion does not refund an App Store purchase. Support records are retained only as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations.

Your choices and contact

You can edit your setup, disable reminders, sign out, or delete your account. For access, correction, deletion help, or privacy questions, use our support form. Your data may be processed in the United States and in locations used by our service providers.

Updates

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the effective date.
