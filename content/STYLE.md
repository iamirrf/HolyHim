# HolyHim voice

Scripture comes first. Quote the World English Bible exactly, including punctuation.
Explain the original setting before offering a present-day connection. Keep God's
promises in context; do not promise health, money, a relationship or an easy outcome.

Speak plainly and warmly to a man with an ordinary, complicated life — strength,
courage, discipline, integrity, leadership, providing, brotherhood, and peace
under pressure. Do not assume marriage, fatherhood, employment, health or a
particular age. Honour grief, safety, boundaries and professional care. Prayer
never replaces practical help.
Use concrete moments, varied sentences, and one small invitation without guilt.
Do not put invented first-person speeches into God's mouth.

Four screens: Scripture; meaning (30–45 words); reflection/heart (30–45);
prayer (30–50, ending Amen.). Keep one useful invitation within the reflection.
The legacy holdOn (8–18 words) remains a short summary for compatibility and
is not rendered as an extra screen. Aim for 120–170 displayed words including Scripture; long
verbatim passages may exceed this. Teasers are at most 90 characters, cut at a word.

Avoid: tapestry, delve, navigate, embrace, unlock, beacon, testament to,
in today's fast-paced world, it's important to remember, transformative journey,
sacred dance, gentle reminder. No repeated filler paragraphs or stock prayers.
Keep suffering visible. Prefer an honest question to a confident prediction.

Editorial batches are sequential, at most 28 days. The automated validator is a
mechanical gate; passing it does not substitute for theological and voice review.
