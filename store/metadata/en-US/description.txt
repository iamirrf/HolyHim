A word to stand on, every morning.

HolyHim is a daily Bible app for young men. One short lesson a day, about five minutes, in plain words. No hype, no lectures, no subscription.

EVERY LESSON, FOUR CARDS
• Scripture: the World English Bible, word for word.
• What it means: the setting and the point, simply explained.
• For your life: one honest step you can take today.
• Prayer: a short prayer. Then hold to say Amen.

THE ROAD
Walk through the Bible a day at a time, chapter by chapter, on a path you can see: Joshua's courage, Joseph's integrity, David and Goliath, Daniel's line in the sand, the Psalms, Proverbs on work and anger, the Sermon on the Mount, Paul's letters to young Timothy, and the hope of Revelation. Lessons speak honestly about the things men face: temptation, anger, fear, loneliness, pressure, failure, money, friendship, fathers and purpose.

BROTHERS
Share a word from today's reading, ask for prayer, or post a win. Say Amen to each other, reply, and climb the board by streak or days read. Every post is checked before it appears, and you can report or block anyone.

STAY ON TRACK
• A daily reminder at the time you choose.
• Streaks with grace days, so one missed day does not wipe you out.
• Nineteen struck medallions to earn along the way. Spin them.
• Save the verses that hit home and share them as clean cards.
• Home screen widget with today's verse.
• Eight themes, from First Watch dark to Limestone light.

MADE FOR YOU
A few quick questions at the start (what you need most, where you are in life, how well you know the Bible) shape your reminders and your welcome. Your progress syncs across devices when you sign in with Apple, Google or email.

SIMPLE, HONEST PRICING
The first seven days are free. After that, one price, once, unlocks every lesson for life. No subscription and no ads.

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (public domain).
