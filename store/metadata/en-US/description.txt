A word to stand on, every morning.

HolyHim is a daily Bible app for young men. One short lesson a day, about five minutes, in plain words. No hype, no lectures and no ads.

EVERY LESSON, FOUR CARDS
• Scripture: the World English Bible, word for word.
• What it means: the setting and the point, simply explained.
• For your life: one honest step you can take today.
• Prayer: a short prayer. Then hold to say Amen.

THE ROAD
Walk through the Bible a day at a time, chapter by chapter, on a road you can see: Joshua's courage, Joseph's integrity, David and Goliath, Daniel's line in the sand, the Psalms, Proverbs on work and anger, the Sermon on the Mount, Paul's letters to young Timothy, and the hope of Revelation. 365 lessons speak honestly about the things men face: temptation, anger, fear, loneliness, pressure, failure, money, friendship, fathers and purpose.

BROTHERS
Share a word from today's reading, ask for prayer, or post a win. Say Amen to each other, reply, and climb the board by streak or days read. Every post is checked before it appears, and you can report or block anyone.

STAY ON TRACK
• A daily note at the time you choose, with a verse in it.
• Streaks with grace days, so one missed day does not wipe you out.
• Nineteen struck medallions to earn along the way.
• Save the verses that hit home and share them as clean cards.
• A home screen widget with today's verse.
• Eight themes with matching app icons, from First Watch dark to Limestone light.

MADE FOR YOU
A short welcome asks what you need most, where you are in life and how well you know the Bible. Your answers shape your reminders and your first week. Your progress syncs across devices when you sign in with Apple, Google or email.

MEMBERSHIP
HolyHim membership opens every lesson. New members can start with a free week. Membership is one yearly price, shown in your currency before you confirm. Payment is charged to your Apple account when you confirm, or when the free week ends. It renews automatically each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it any time in your App Store account settings.

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible (public domain).
