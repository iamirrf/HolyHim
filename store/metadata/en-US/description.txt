A little faith. A little courage. Every day.

HolyHer is a daily Bible companion for women, with 365 complete lessons that bring Scripture into the ordinary moments of life. Read one passage, understand the reason behind its encouragement, and carry a little hope into your day.

FIVE CARDS, ONE GENTLE LESSON
Move through Verse, Confidence, Reason, Hope, and Carry. Each lesson begins with an exact World English Bible quotation, followed by original writing addressed directly to you. A separately labeled devotional reflection uses a literary first-person voice inspired by God's care; it is distinct from the quoted Scripture.

A YEAR AT YOUR OWN RHYTHM
Your first lesson opens when your lifetime journey begins. One more lesson becomes available each calendar day. Previously unlocked lessons stay available, so missed days accumulate and you can return to them when you are ready. Revisit any unlocked lesson without losing your place.

MAKE THE ROUTINE YOURS
Choose your Bible familiarity, focus, and daily routine in a thoughtful guided setup. Set an optional reminder, track your progress and activity streak, and find a practical reflection to carry into everyday life.

YOUR JOURNEY, ACROSS DEVICES
Sign in with Apple, Google, or email to sync your setup and reading progress between iPhone and iPad. Previously unlocked lessons can be read offline after setup. Reconnect to sync progress and unlock new days.

ONE PURCHASE, LIFETIME ACCESS
Launch price: US$1.99 for the 365-lesson edition. Your local App Store price is shown before you purchase. Pay once, with no subscription or automatic renewal. Restore Purchases is available in the app.

HolyHer includes no advertising. Its encouragement is grounded in Scripture and offers room for honest questions, patient growth, and everyday faith.
