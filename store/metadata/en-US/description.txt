Walk with God, one day at a time.

HolyHer is a free daily Scripture companion for women. Make a little room for God in the full days, the uncertain days, and the ordinary days. Follow a 365-day journey through five quiet reading cards, usually two to three minutes a day.

FIVE CARDS, A LITTLE HOPE
Each reading includes an exact World English Bible passage, its biblical meaning, everyday encouragement, a truth to carry, and a short original prayer. There are no quizzes, timers, or required written reflections.

A YEAR AT YOUR OWN PACE
One new reading opens each journey day. Previously unlocked readings stay available for rereading or catching up. A missed day never takes away a badge you have earned.

YOUR QUIET PLACE
Choose Pearl, Rose, Lavender, Sage, Dawn, or Midnight. Adjust reading size, save meaningful verses, and share Scripture as text or a beautiful image. Optional personalization makes the introduction feel more relevant without changing the Bible text or lesson order.

SMALL STEPS, LASTING KEEPSAKES
See your completed-reading calendar, current and longest reading streaks, and permanent badges at 1, 7, 30, 90, 180, and 365 distinct readings. Rereading can support a daily streak while distinct lesson progress stays accurate.

A GENTLE INVITATION
Choose an optional iPhone or iPad reminder with a Scripture excerpt and reference. Tap it to open that reading's Scripture card. Notification permission is specific to each device, and reminders can be disabled at any time.

YOUR WALK, TOGETHER
Sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Sync your setup, themes, saved moments, and progress across supported devices. Previously unlocked readings remain available offline after successful setup. Reconnect to synchronize changes and unlock new days.

The complete HolyHer 365 journey is free to access. There is no advertising or current purchase flow. Original explanations and prayers are presented separately from the public-domain World English Bible text.
