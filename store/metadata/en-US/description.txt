Walk with God, one day at a time.

HolyHim is a daily Scripture companion for women. Make a little room for God in the full days, the uncertain days, and the ordinary days. Follow a 365-day journey through four quiet reading screens, at a pace you choose.

FOUR CARDS, A DAILY MOMENT
Each reading includes an exact World English Bible passage, its biblical meaning, a reflection, and a short original prayer. There are no quizzes, timers, or required written reflections.

A YEAR AT YOUR OWN PACE
One new reading opens each journey day. Previously unlocked readings stay available for rereading or catching up. A missed day never takes away a badge you have earned.

YOUR QUIET PLACE
Choose among eight themes. Adjust reading size, save meaningful verses, and share Scripture as text or a beautiful image. Optional personalization makes the introduction feel more relevant without changing the Bible text or lesson order.

SMALL STEPS, LASTING KEEPSAKES
See your completed-reading calendar, current and longest reading streaks, and permanent badges at milestones across the 365-day walk. Rereading can support a daily streak while distinct lesson progress stays accurate.

A GENTLE INVITATION
Choose an optional iPhone or iPad reminder with a Scripture excerpt and reference. Tap it to open that reading's Scripture card. Notification permission is specific to each device, and reminders can be disabled at any time.

YOUR WALK, TOGETHER
Sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Sync your setup, themes, saved moments, and progress across supported devices. Previously unlocked readings remain available offline after successful setup. Reconnect to synchronize changes and unlock new days.

HolyHim offers one lifetime purchase, with localized pricing shown before confirmation, plus restoration for eligible purchases and retained access. There is no subscription or advertising. Original explanations and prayers are presented separately from the public-domain World English Bible text.
