Development version 1.2.0 (4). HolyHer is a free 365-day Scripture journey. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Setup has three optional personalization steps and an actual Day 1 preview. Each reading has exactly five cards: Scripture, Meaning, Encouragement, Hope, and Prayer. Visit all five and tap Finish my reading. No purchase, quiz, timer, or written response is required. One additional reading unlocks each journey calendar day; previously unlocked readings remain available offline after setup.

Walk, Saved, and Profile retain tab state. Six themes, reading size, reminders, export, reset, and account deletion are available in Profile and Settings. Native reminders carry a WEB excerpt and open the quoted reading. The web app edits reminder preference without browser notification delivery.
