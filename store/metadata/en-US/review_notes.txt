HolyHim is a daily Bible app for young men: one short lesson a day (Scripture, what it means, for your life, a prayer), then the reader holds a button to say Amen.

Sign-in: Sign in with Apple, Google, or email and password. Any of these creates an account in a few seconds.

Free week and purchase: Days 1 to 7 are free. Day 8 onward requires the one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase "HolyHim Lifetime" (holyhim_lifetime). There is no subscription. The offer appears at the end of onboarding ("Unlock for life" or "Start my 7 free days"), from Profile, and on Day 8. Restore Purchase is on the offer screen and in Profile. Lessons open one per calendar day, so the Day 8 lock is reached on the eighth day, but the purchase can be made at any time from the offer or Profile.

Account deletion: Profile > Account > Delete account. This permanently deletes the account, progress and saved verses.

Notifications are optional. Permission is requested only when the reader chooses a reminder time.

Scripture quotations use the public-domain World English Bible. All lesson text is original.

HolyHim is its own product, separate from our app HolyHer (for women): it has its own 365 lessons written for young men, its own reading plan, design, artwork, onboarding and listing.
