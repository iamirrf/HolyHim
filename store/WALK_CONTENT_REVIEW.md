# Five-card reading review — November 2, 2026

Codex reviewed the retained biblical context, everyday application, and hope for all 365 readings in numbered batches, together with the shared expansion prose, original prayer variants, and Day 1's complete rewrite. The review covers the reading-only edition, not the retained legacy quiz catalog. It does not claim an independent human or denominational review.

The original lesson titles, sequence, and references remain intact. The Scripture card is the exact public-domain WEB passage from the independently verified pinned corpus. The Meaning card retains the original context; the Encouragement card retains a practical connection; Hope retains the original assurance. All first-person devotional speech attributed to God and all reflection questions from the legacy closing cards are omitted from this edition. Prayer is original prayer addressed to God, followed by a closing takeaway.

The review distinguished historical and covenant settings from universal personal guarantees, wisdom sayings from financial contracts, particular healings from promises of recovery, lament from failed faith, and forgiveness from the removal of accountability. Original context in passages such as Jeremiah 29:11, Psalm 91:1, Matthew 15:28, 1 Corinthians 10:13, Hebrews 12:11, and Revelation 21:4 remains explicit.

Optional writing activities in days 54, 94, 111, 119, 136, 146, 283, and 320 were replaced with reading-only encouragement. No response field, writing task, answer scoring, or timer is used in the reader. Practical suggestions remain optional prose, not completion requirements.

The expansion paragraphs and prayer variants recur across the curriculum; the lesson-specific passages, contexts, applications, and assurances remain distinct. This is an adaptation of the retained curriculum, not a claim of 365 separately commissioned essays.

`content/reading-review.json` records the reviewer, scope, time, and a hash of every reviewed five-card body. Changing a body invalidates its review hash. Structural checks do not grant review. `tool/build_readings.py --check` verifies the review evidence, card structure, word counts, exact Scripture, retained identity, and generated app/sample outputs.

Artwork remains a separate review process. No Gemini images have been generated or approved without the missing API credential.
